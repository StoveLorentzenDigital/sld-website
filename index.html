Our Philosophy

"Tools should be as simple as pencils. Anyone can pick them up and begin making their mark."

We spent years in video game production, where art, engineering, and design slam into each other constantly. As technical artists we sat right in the middle: building the pipelines and tools that let artists actually do art, instead of wrestling with software.

That work taught us something that applies far beyond games: the real bottleneck in creative digital work is almost never the technology. It's the friction between the people using it. Teams miscommunicating. Tools that break concentration. Processes designed around the pipeline rather than the people in it. We know how to spot those problems and fix them.

Now we bring that same thinking to studios, agencies, and product teams outside of games. If your people spend more time fighting their tools than doing their best work, talk to us.
